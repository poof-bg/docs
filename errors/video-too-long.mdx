## Reasons and How to Fix

### Duration Exceeds 5 Minutes

Poof probes the video before accepting the job and rejects anything longer than 5 minutes (300 seconds). Nothing is billed.

**To fix:**
1. **Trim** the clip to the part you need
2. **Split** longer videos into segments of up to 5 minutes, submit each as its own job, and join the results

### Trimming and Splitting with ffmpeg

```bash
# Keep the first 5 minutes
ffmpeg -i long.mp4 -t 300 -c copy part1.mp4

# Split into 5 minute segments (part_000.mp4, part_001.mp4, ...)
ffmpeg -i long.mp4 -c copy -map 0 -segment_time 300 -f segment -reset_timestamps 1 part_%03d.mp4
```

`-c copy` avoids re-encoding, so it is fast and lossless. Cuts land on the nearest keyframe, so segments may be a second or so longer or shorter than requested; keep a small margin below 300 seconds if the exact cut matters.

### Credits

Each segment is billed separately at 5 credits per second of source video, rounded up per job, so splitting a 7 minute clip into a 5 minute and a 2 minute job costs the same as it would in one job. See [Credits](/api-reference/video/remove-background#credits).
